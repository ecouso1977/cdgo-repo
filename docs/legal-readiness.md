# US Legal Readiness Notes

This is not legal advice. It is a build blocker list.

## Current blocker

The stated plan is a US fundraising token tied to project participation. That can look like an investment contract, which means public sale or exchange trading may require securities analysis, exemptions, transfer restrictions, disclosures, KYC/AML controls, and counsel-reviewed distribution terms.

## Before any public sale or exchange outreach

1. Get US securities counsel to classify the token model.
2. Decide whether the token is utility-only, governance-only, or part of a regulated offering.
3. Prepare written tokenomics, vesting, treasury controls, insider allocation, and participant rights.
4. Put sanctions screening, AML, and jurisdiction restrictions in place if any transfer restrictions apply.
5. Commission a smart-contract audit and preserve the report for marketplaces.
6. Prepare tax, accounting, privacy-policy, and terms-of-sale documents.
7. Avoid marketing language that frames the token as an investment return vehicle.

## What this scaffold is safe for today

- Local development
- Testnet deployment
- Internal review of tokenomics and governance mechanics
- Audit preparation

## What this scaffold does not solve

- Securities registration or exemption analysis
- Broker-dealer, ATS, or marketplace legal requirements
- Exchange due diligence or listing approval
- KYC/AML operations
